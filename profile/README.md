# SIG-WUS

A community effort exploring a Special Interest Group in wearable ultrasound:
researchers, clinicians, and industry working toward shared standards, events,
education, and open exchange.

**Website:** https://sig-wus.github.io

## Structure

| Working Group (WG) | Focus |
|---|---|
| WG1 Standards & Regulatory Affairs | White paper, safety guidelines, IEC / FDA / IEEE SA liaison |
| WG2 Events & Education | IUS special sessions, courses, summer school, YP, Dresden 2027 |
| WG3 Industry & Clinical Translation | Industry liaison, showcases, clinical adoption pathways |
| Open Exchange Platform | Cross-WG project: open hardware & software, reference designs, datasets, benchmarking |

---

SIG-WUS is a community initiative.
